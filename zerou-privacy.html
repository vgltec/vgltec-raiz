Privacy policy

Zerou: Debt Payoff Planner (Android, com.vgltec.quitardividas). Last updated: November 15, 2026. Versão em português.

Who we are

Zerou is an app by VGL Tecnologia (Brazil), the controller of the data described in this policy. For any privacy question, email vgl.tecsp@gmail.com.

Zerou is a personal planning tool. It does not offer loans, does not negotiate with creditors, does not check your credit and does not connect to your bank account.

Your debts stay on your phone

The app has no sign-up and no account. Your debts, balances, interest rates, logged payments and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. You can delete everything at any time by deleting your debts in the app, clearing the app's data in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you use the backup feature, the app asks for your permission on Google's screen and saves a copy of your data in a hidden app folder inside your own Google Drive (the "drive.appdata" permission). This permission only gives access to files created by Zerou itself: the app cannot see any other file in your Drive. The backup does not go through our servers and we have no access to it. You can delete it in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → Zerou → Delete hidden app data.

Reminders

Due date reminders are scheduled on your phone. No information leaves your device for this.

Data collected by Google services

To show ads, understand how the app is used and process purchases, Zerou uses these services from Google LLC:

  • Google AdMob (ads, free version only): may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), device information and ad interactions, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud.
  • Firebase / Google Analytics (usage statistics): logs events such as opening the app, adding a debt, logging a payment, paying off a debt, viewing the schedule, creating the PDF and visiting the Premium screen, plus device model, Android version, language, approximate country and city (from your IP address) and app identifiers. We do not send debt names, amounts, interest rates or payments: events only carry counts (for example, "3 debts").
  • Google Play Billing (purchases): payment for Zerou Premium is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details; the app only receives a purchase confirmation.

How Google uses this data is described in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form the first time you open it, where you choose whether to allow personalized ads and measurement. You can change your choice later from the app menu, under "Privacy settings". Without consent you may still see non-personalized ads.

Legal bases and purposes

  • Performance of the service: running the app, unlocking Premium and making the backup you asked for.
  • Consent: personalized ads and measurement, where required by law.
  • Legitimate interest: aggregated statistics to improve the app, security and fraud prevention.

Retention

Data on your phone and the backup in your Drive are kept until you delete them. Google Analytics data is kept for the period configured in the service (at most 14 months for user-level data) and then deleted automatically. AdMob data follows Google's rules.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's LGPD and similar laws, you can request access, correction, deletion, portability or objection to the processing of your data by emailing vgl.tecsp@gmail.com. Because the app has no account, we may ask for the app identifier to find the data. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. We do not sell your personal information. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

Zerou is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The last updated date is shown at the top; important changes will also be announced in the app or on its Google Play page.
